What guides us

Three foundations

01

Nature

Materials come from local and natural sources. The building knows its place. Material reality, not sustainability aesthetics. Where wood, stone, earth, or plant fibres exist locally, they carry knowledge about how a place behaves.

02

Human Made

Architecture emerges from the people involved. Every participant brings something: craft, labour, ideas, knowledge. A local craftsperson's understanding of timber is as essential as an engineer's calculations. Making is central.

03

Work & Live Together

Building and inhabiting happen side by side. The cluster is not just constructed — it is lived in from the start, connecting skills, traditions, and experiences across generations and geographies.

The vision

Work together.
Live together.

Weaving all together — connecting people, skills, traditions and experiences.

The first project is a cluster at the field edge in Indonesia. A group of buildings built together and inhabited together — workshop, living, gathering, and growing around a shared space. A place for making, learning, and living.
